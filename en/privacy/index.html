Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Naskh collects no data about you. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no servers of ours. What you copy stays on your devices.

Who we are

Naskh is a clipboard manager for Mac, iPhone, and iPad, made by an independent developer, Besher Sheikh Othman. This policy covers the Naskh apps and their extensions (the keyboard, widgets, and share extension), and this website.

Data we collect

None. Naskh sends us nothing about you, how you use it, or what you copy. The app contains no analytics, ads, or tracking of any kind, and it never asks you to create an account.

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us crash reports that don’t identify you, under Apple’s policy and with your permission.

What you copy

Naskh saves what you copy — text, links, images, colors, and more — only on your device, inside the app’s own storage. You decide how long it’s kept, and you can delete any clip or clear your whole history at any time. Deleting the app deletes its history from the device.

What Naskh never saves

On the Mac, Naskh never saves:

  • Anything you copy from password managers, such as Apple Passwords, 1Password, and Bitwarden.
  • Anything the source app marks as confidential or temporary.
  • Anything you copy from apps you add to “Excluded apps” in Naskh’s settings.

iCloud sync

Sync is an optional Naskh Pro feature, and it only runs if you turn it on. Your history and pinboards then sync between your devices through your own private iCloud database (CloudKit), tied to your Apple Account. We can’t see this data, it never passes through any other server, and it’s covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

You can turn sync off at any time in Naskh’s settings, and delete Naskh’s data from iCloud in your device’s Apple Account settings (iCloud → Manage Storage).

Link previews

When you open the preview of a link you copied, your device fetches the page’s title and image directly from that website using Apple’s LinkPresentation framework, just like a browser would. That request never goes through us.

Permissions

  • Accessibility (Mac): Naskh asks for it only to send ⌘V and paste what you pick into the app you’re working in. It doesn’t read your screen or what you type.
  • Allow Full Access (keyboard on iPhone and iPad): the Naskh keyboard needs it only to read your history stored on the device. The keyboard doesn’t log what you type and never sends anything anywhere.
  • Paste from Other Apps (iPhone and iPad): Naskh reads the clipboard only to save what you copy to your history.

Purchases

Apple handles all Naskh Pro purchases and subscriptions through the App Store. We receive no information about who you are or how you pay; the app only learns whether you have an active subscription.

Children

Naskh collects no data from anyone, children included.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking. It’s hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts; like any web server, these two services may log your IP address, under their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll publish the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact
